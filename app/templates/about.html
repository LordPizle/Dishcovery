About Dishcovery

Your AI-powered food recommendation system. Find restaurants near you based on what you crave.

How it works

  1. Search – Enter what you're craving (pizza, sushi, vegan, etc.)
  2. Location – Allow location access so we can find spots near you
  3. Discover – Browse results with photos, ratings and opening hours

AI Chat Assistant

Click the chat icon in the corner to ask our AI about cuisines, dishes or dietary preferences. Get instant food recommendations and tips.

Powered by

Dishcovery uses Google Places to find restaurants. Location data is used only to show nearby results and is not stored.
