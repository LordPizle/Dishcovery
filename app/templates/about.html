About Dishcovery

Dishcovery helps you find restaurants based on what you are craving and where you want to eat. Search by dish or cuisine, use your location or an address and get practical results fast.

How it works

  1. Search your craving - Enter a dish or cuisine (for example: pizza, sushi, vegan, tacos).
  2. Choose where to search - Use your current location or enter an address or city.
  3. Refine with filters - Adjust distance, number of results, minimum rating and open now.
  4. Pick a place - Compare photos, ratings, opening status and distance then open it in Google Maps.

How ranking works

Dishcovery supports three sort modes: Distance, Rating, and Best match. Best match uses a text-based recommender that compares your query with restaurant information and ranks results by relevance.

Under the hood, Best match uses TF-IDF and cosine similarity with query expansion for related terms (for example, matching "ramen" with noodle or Japanese places). Relevance is combined with distance and rating to produce practical results.

Chat assistant

The chat assistant is quick and practical. You can ask about cuisines, diets and ideas (for example: "vegan options" or "date night").

It can also trigger real searches such as "pizza in Manchester" or "sushi near me", then return restaurant suggestions using the same ranking logic as Find Food.

Data and sources

Restaurant data comes from Google Places. Address lookups use geocoding so city and address searches work smoothly.

Location is used only to answer your current search and improve nearby recommendations.

What Dishcovery does not do

Dishcovery does not place orders or handle delivery payments. It focuses on helping you decide where to eat and then takes you to Maps to continue.
